<h1>Abertay Third Year Group Project</h1>
<p>I worked in a group with 7 other students across different disciplines to create a 2D platformer called Dungeon Heist where the goal is to climb to the top of a dungeon while outrunning lava rising from the bottom of the dungeon and avoiding the dragon on the way up. I worked in the unity engine and focused on creating the player movement including the ability to throw a blade into walls and bounce of of it for extra height.<br>
